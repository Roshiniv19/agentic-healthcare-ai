Life-Saving Protocols

Step-by-Step First Aid Guides

Evidence-based instructions while awaiting first responders.

Step 1: Check & Call

Tap shoulder firmly: "Are you okay?". If no response, call 112 (emergency) or 108 (ambulance) immediately and send someone for medical help or an AED.

Step 2: Hand Position

Place the heel of one hand in the center of the chest. Interlock your other hand on top with elbows locked straight.

Step 3: Push Hard & Fast

Compress at 100-120 BPM (to the beat of "Stayin' Alive"), at least 2 inches deep. Allow chest to recoil between compressions.

  1. Confirm Choking: Ask "Are you choking?". If they cannot speak, cough, or breathe, initiate abdominal thrusts.
  2. Stand Behind: Wrap your arms around the person's waist and lean them slightly forward.
  3. Make a Fist: Place thumb side of fist just above the navel, well below the breastbone.
  4. Inward & Upward Thrusts: Grasp fist with other hand and give quick, upward thrusts until the object is expelled.
